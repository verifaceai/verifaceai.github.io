Synthetic Media Identification Guide

Generative adversarial networks (GANs) and diffusion models leave subtle visual cues. Here is what to analyze when assessing authenticity.

Artifact #1

Eyes & Pupil Geometry

AI models often struggle with fine iris textures, catchlight consistency between both eyes, or produce misshapen pupils.

Artifact #2

Hair Structure & Fine Details

Individual hair strands on synthetic images frequently melt together, blur into the skin, or form helmet-like shapes.

Artifact #3

Ears, Jewelry & Accessories

Asymmetry is common in AI portraits: mismatched earrings, glasses frames that dissolve into the temple, or strange ear folds.

Artifact #4

Background Coherence

Background textures behind synthetic faces often feature surreal blur artifacts, floating objects, or unnatural light sources.
